---
name: Partner account model
description: Partner login requirements and separation from dating-member accounts.
---

Partner accounts can be created in the admin panel with a chosen username and password or generated credentials, then provided directly to the partner. Partner access does not require an email address. A partner may separately register as a standard dating member using their own email. Keep partner sign-in and reporting separate from member profiles, referrals, and credits.

**Why:** The user wants admin-managed partner credentials without email because a partner can use their email independently to join the dating site.

**How to apply:** Preserve this split when changing partner onboarding, authentication, or dashboards. Do not add email invitations or account linking unless requested.
